Primary Finding

1. Typing-Invariant Spatial Sparsity

All 6 CD274–PDCD1 combinations that were falsely flagged as significant under cluster-marginal means collapsed to non-significant under the edge null, with an observed edge score of 0.000000 (p = 1.000). Zero physical contact edges connect CD274+ to PDCD1+ cells under either typing scheme (typing-invariant across two independent cell-typing schemes: per-cell argmax and Leiden-consensus). This provides direct evidence that marginal scoring manufactures phantom significance for uncontacted cells.

Core Paracrine Axis

2. Dominant Fibroblast→Immune Stability

Under both typing schemes, fibroblast→immune (CXCL12→CXCR4) is the undisputed top communication channel: observed edge score 20.21, 1,094 physical contact edges, and z = +39.57 (vs. 16.50 and z = +29.51 under per-cell typing). Paracrine communication between stromal fibroblasts and infiltrating immune cells is biologically and statistically robust.

Methodological Caveat

3. Granularity Sensitivity in Rare Lineages

Minor relative rank variations among secondary CXCL12–CXCR4 interactions are sensitive to cell-typing granularity. At the default Leiden clustering resolution (res = 0.5, the only resolution actually clustered in this pipeline), rare endothelial cells (N = 49, 0.68%) are absorbed into larger epithelial/fibroblast clusters rather than forming their own cluster. Secondary rank shifts stem from clustering granularity rather than spatial scoring instability.
