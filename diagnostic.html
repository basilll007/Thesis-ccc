CD274–PDCD1 Zero-Variance Diagnostic

Why did edge_null_std == 0.000000 for all CD274–PDCD1 pairs? PDCD1 was detected in only 5 of 7,163 cells (0.07%) (0 immune, 0 endothelial, 1 epithelial, 4 fibroblast). CD274 was detected in 53 of 7,163 cells (0.74%). Crucially, across all 42,978 graph edges, exactly 0 edges connect a CD274+ cell to a PDCD1+ cell. Because node expressions are fixed during label shuffling, the edge product vector is identically zero in every permutation, guaranteeing zero variance.
