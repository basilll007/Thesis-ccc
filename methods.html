2026-09-19 · BiLSTM Spatial Feature Extractor & GNN Ablation

Deep Learning Gate Check: Pre-Registered Gate FAILED (AUC 0.7628 vs 0.9799)

Trained a 2-layer BiLSTM autoencoder to reconstruct center cell expression from k=10 nearest neighbor sequences. Shuffle test proved the BiLSTM is order-sensitive (13.98% displacement). Concatenating BiLSTM-50 with PCA-50 collapsed BiLSTM achieved cell-type silhouette of 0.4287 (ratio 2.9051 vs PCA-50's 0.1476; 20.10× higher than the spatial (x,y) control of 0.0213), confirming its silhouette advantage reflects local transcriptomic smoothing rather than a spatial-autocorrelation artifact. However, this spatial smoothing destroys the local cell-specific variance required for GraphSAGE edge link prediction. See GNN Ablation Dashboard.

2026-09-16 · Self-Supervised GNN Link Prediction & Edge Confidence

GraphSAGE Link Prediction Gate Passed on PCA-50 (AUC 0.9799), scGPT Failed (0.7011)

2-layer GraphSAGE trained self-supervised on the 42,978-edge spatial graph via dot-product link prediction. scGPT embeddings failed the gate (AUC 0.7011); PCA-50 passed decisively (AUC 0.9799). Subsequent edge-confidence filtering preserved ranking (rho=0.953) but diluted top z-scores.

2026-09-15 · scGPT Zero-Shot Embedding Baseline

Foundation-Model Gate Check: Passed Narrowly, Ground Truth Not Reproduced

Ran the pretrained scGPT whole-human checkpoint zero-shot (no fine-tuning) on the 274/280 panel genes present in its vocabulary. Zero-shot silhouette (0.136) reached only 92.1% of a PCA baseline (0.148) on the same genes/cells — passed the pre-registered ≥90% gate, narrowly. The resulting embedding-similarity communication score did not correlate with the existing spatial-edge edge_z_score ranking (Spearman ρ = −0.002, p = 0.99); CD274→PDCD1 pairs, which have 0 physical spatial edges, showed high (not low) embedding similarity. See scGPT Baseline.

2026-09-13 · Scientific Framing Realignment

Elevation of CD274–PDCD1 Sparsity & Cell-Typing Granularity Caveat

Resolution sweep ($res = 0.5$ to $2.0$) demonstrated that rare endothelial cells ($N=49$, 0.68%) remain merged into larger clusters without artificial over-clustering. Formally realigned the thesis architecture under Option 1: promoted CD274–PDCD1 zero-edge spatial non-contact as the primary typing-robust proof of method utility, and caveated CXCL12–CXCR4 secondary rank variations as sensitive to rare-lineage community granularity.
