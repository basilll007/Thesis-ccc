The Spatial Verdict Flip Matrix:
9 False Positives Unmasked

Under cluster-marginal scoring (Squidpy default), 17 of 22 candidate interactions were reported as "statistically significant". Once communication is evaluated strictly across physical edges in the tissue's 6-NN spatial connectivity graph under a 500-permutation null, 9 of these 17 interactions collapse to non-significant.
