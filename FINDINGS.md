All workstreams are expression-data analyses: results are audits, associations or predictive models, **not causal, mechanistic or clinical claims**. ## What holds across the workstreams
1. **Composition and zonation explain most "communication" signal.** Edge-based CCC scores mostly mirror cell-type products (v2). Constrained nulls are fooled by zonation (v3). Neighbourhood prediction is driven by same-type neighbours (chain, model v1).
2. **Power is the binding constraint.** The Q audit (v3) and the chain test (chain v1) were both calibrated by simulation and found unable to detect effects of plausible size at the available cell counts. The model learning curve suggests about +0.06 R2 per tenfold more cells.
3. **The only consistent cross-type lead is CAF <-> macrophage.** It appears as an exploratory adjacency association (chain claim C10), as the largest other-type contribution to macrophage-state prediction (model v1), and as a model-based hypothesis (CXCL12-high CAFs <-> C1Q/SELENOP-type macrophages). All three come from the same kind of data. It is a candidate for experimental testing, not a finding. **Update (model v2):** the model-based part of this lead is explained by segmentation spillover under a pre-declared control. On the best model the cross-type share is about zero. What remains is an exploratory, post-hoc observation: macrophages touching CAFs have more C1Q/CD163/MRC1 RNA than average-CAF mixing predicts. It is a candidate for a spillover-free experiment (`WETLAB_BRIEF_v1.md`), not a finding.
4. **The external cohorts support consistency, not replication.** GSE310352 patient identity, independence and um/px scale are unverified.
5. **Hypoxia is an independently validated prognostic association, while immune/stromal signatures are not.** The 12-gene hypoxia signature significantly associates with shorter overall survival across independent microarray cohorts (pooled HR 1.38 [1.07, 1.79] per SD, one-sided p = 0.0072, I2 = 0.0 %), matching the discovery effect in TCGA (HR 1.32) and CPTAC (HR 1.43) to yield a 4-cohort pooled HR of 1.367 [1.191, 1.570] (p = 4.4e-6, I2 = 0.0 %). Conversely, macrophage (C1Q, SPP1), CAF, and T-cell proxy signatures show no prognostic association (all q > 0.80), demonstrating specificity to hypoxia rather than general immune or stromal cell abundance. (Associative only; patient-level inference; no causal or therapeutic claim.)

## Status
- **Branches:** `pdac-v2`, `thesis-v3`, `chain-v1`, `model-v1` and `translational-v1` are all merged into `main`.
- **Translational work:** the survival models were run in model v2 and independently validated in Phase B on `framework-v1`. Hypoxia is independently validated (pooled HR 1.38, p = 0.0072); exploratory signatures are null.
- **Remaining limits:** segmentation spillover is now estimated (model v2: 23 % excess CAF-marker RNA in touching macrophages), but not removed at the segmentation level. T-cell states are largely below the CosMx background.
- **Branch `model-v2`:** spillover control, cross-type share on aggregated models and survival; not merged into `main`.
- **Branch `framework-v1`:** installable `ccc_audit` package, unit & reproduction test suite (10/10 passed), end-to-end demo on Mendeley PDAC-SMI, and Phase B independent hypoxia survival validation. Not merged into `main`.
