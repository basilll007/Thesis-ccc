## Abstract

Cell–cell communication (CCC) inference in spatial transcriptomics is typically ranked using cluster-mean ligand–receptor (L–R) scores that never reference the spatial graph itself. On a 10x Xenium human breast dataset, restricted to the only two curated L–R pairs whose ligand and receptor both survive the 280-gene targeted panel (CXCL12–CXCR4, CD274–PDCD1 — the other 18 of 20 curated pairs were untestable a priori), we show that compositional scoring can manufacture significance for interactions with **zero physical spatial support**. Every CD274→PDCD1 sender–receiver combination flagged significant under compositional scoring (4 of 4 under per-cell marker typing, 6 of 6 under an independent Leiden-consensus typing) collapses to an edge-based score of exactly 0.000 under a spatial-edge null: not one of 42,978 directed spatial-graph edges connects a CD274-expressing cell to a PDCD1-expressing cell, in either typing scheme. This survives Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction (all 9 verdict flips at q≤0.003 for the compositional null; corresponding edge-null q≥0.05, median q=1.00) and replicates identically across both typing methods. In contrast, the CXCL12→CXCR4 axis shows an instability that is itself typing-dependent: the top compositionally-ranked interaction falls from rank 1 to rank 8 of 16 (89% z-score reduction) under per-cell typing, but only from rank 2 to rank 3 of 9 under Leiden-consensus typing — a mild shift, with 0 of 9 combinations losing significance versus 5 of 16 under per-cell typing. A zero-shot scGPT embedding baseline narrowly clears a PCA quality gate (silhouette ratio 0.92) but does not recover this spatial-contact signal (Spearman ρ≈0 vs. edge z-score; CD274→PDCD1 pairs show *high*, not low, embedding similarity despite zero physical contact) — evidence that spatial-graph-explicit scoring captures structure a general-purpose transcriptomic embedding misses. CCC pipelines should report significance under spatially-explicit nulls, and rank-instability claims should be checked against more than one cell-typing scheme before being treated as general.

## Motivation

Spatial transcriptomics is used to study how tumor microenvironment cells communicate through physical proximity, yet the dominant scoring convention in widely used tools — CellPhoneDB-style cluster-mean expression products, as implemented in `squidpy.gr.ligrec` (Palla et al. 2022) — computes a statistic mathematically identical to what non-spatial scRNA-seq would give: it averages expression within cell-type clusters and never touches the spatial connectivity graph. This creates a validity gap between what the score claims to measure (spatially-supported communication) and what it actually measures (cluster co-expression, independent of location). We quantify this gap using a controlled ablation — same interactions, same permutation engine, differing only in whether the statistic conditions on the spatial graph — and additionally test whether conclusions about *which* interactions are unstable are themselves robust to cell-typing method, since typing choice is rarely treated as an experimental variable in CCC benchmarking.

## Data and Methods

**Panel coverage (scope statement).** Of the 20 curated L–R pairs, only 9 of the 37 unique genes involved are on the 10x Xenium 280-gene targeted breast panel used here, and only **2 of the 20 pairs** — CXCL12–CXCR4 and CD274–PDCD1 — have *both* ligand and receptor on-panel. The other 18 were untestable a priori, a hard constraint of the commercial panel design, not a post hoc filtering choice. All results below concern these 2 pairs: 22 sender–receiver combinations under per-cell typing, 18 under Leiden-consensus typing (effective n=2 L–R pairs).

**Dataset.** Public 10x Genomics Xenium Human Breast panel, 2-FOV bundle (CC BY 4.0). After QC (min_counts=10, min_cells=3): 7,163 cells × 280 genes. Spatial graph: squidpy generic k-NN, k=6, no self-loops (42,978 directed edges).

**Two independent cell-typing schemes.** (1) *Per-cell*: marker-score argmax, 4 types (epithelial 5,355; fibroblast 1,398; immune 361; endothelial 49). (2) *Leiden-consensus*: cluster-level consensus labels from Leiden clustering at resolution 0.5 (the only resolution clustered and saved in this pipeline), yielding 3 types after endothelial cells (n=49, 0.68%) are absorbed into larger clusters (epithelial 5,756; immune 773; fibroblast 634). Resolution was not tuned to force endothelial separation; absorption at the default resolution is reported as-is.

**Baseline ranking.** The 20 curated L–R pairs, scored with `squidpy.gr.ligrec` cluster-mean statistics, yield 22 nonzero sender–receiver combinations under per-cell typing (16 CXCL12→CXCR4, 6 CD274→PDCD1) and 18 under Leiden-consensus typing (9 each).

**Null model 1 — compositional.** Cell-type labels permuted uniformly at random (N=500, seed=42), preserving cluster sizes; statistic is the cluster-mean L–R product, identical to the non-spatial baseline. This null never queries the spatial graph.

**Null model 2 — spatial-edge.** Same 500 permutations of the same labels, but the statistic is computed over the spatial graph directly: for each permutation, select directed edges (i→j) whose permuted endpoint labels match the sender/receiver type, and average L(i)×R(j) over exactly those edges. This is the only change between the two nulls — same interactions, same seed, same permutation draws — isolating the effect of conditioning on physical adjacency. Both nulls were re-run identically under per-cell and Leiden-consensus typing.

**Multiple-testing correction.** No corrected-significance artifact existed prior to this draft; Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction was computed directly from the empirical p-values in `results/null_model_comparison.csv`, across the 22 per-cell-typing tests, separately per null.

**Zero-shot embedding baseline (negative control).** Pretrained scGPT "whole-human" (Cui et al. 2024) was run zero-shot — no fine-tuning — on the 274/280 panel genes in its vocabulary (97.9% overlap), embedding all 7,163 cells to derive a per-pair embedding-similarity score (mean cosine similarity over the Null-model-2 edges).

**Reproducibility.** Reruns produce byte-identical SHA-256 output hashes; p-values below the 1/500 permutation floor are reported as p<0.002, not p=0.000.

## Results

**Primary finding: CD274→PDCD1 zero-spatial-contact is typing-invariant.** Under compositional scoring, CD274→PDCD1 is flagged significant (p<0.05) in 4 of 6 per-cell-typing combinations and 6 of 6 Leiden-consensus combinations. Under the spatial-edge null, every one collapses to an observed edge score of exactly 0.000 (p=1.000, edge z=0) in **both** typing schemes: not one of 42,978 directed spatial edges connects a CD274-expressing cell to a PDCD1-expressing cell, regardless of typing. This traces to PDCD1's near-absent detection (5 of 7,163 cells, 0.07%). BH-FDR correction across the 22 per-cell-typing tests confirms all 9 verdict flips survive at q≤0.003 for the compositional null, while edge-null q-values remain ≥0.05 throughout (median q=1.00, minimum q=0.185) — not an artifact of uncorrected multiple testing.

**Secondary finding: CXCL12→CXCR4 rank instability is itself sensitive to cell-typing choice.** Under per-cell typing, the top compositionally-ranked interaction, fibroblast→epithelial (z=42.9), falls to rank 8 of 16 under the spatial-edge null (z=4.6), an 89% z-score reduction, and 5 of 16 compositionally-significant combinations lose significance entirely (median z 5.67→0.85, all with 17–360 qualifying edges). Compositional and edge z-scores correlate at only Spearman ρ=0.79 (p=0.0002) across the 16 combinations. Under Leiden-consensus typing, the *same* interaction shows only a mild rank shift — compositional rank 2 to edge rank 3 of 9 (z=45.3→18.6) — and 0 of 9 combinations lose significance; the correlation is markedly tighter (ρ=0.97 vs. 0.79). Edge count does not predict edge z-score under either typing (per-cell: ρ=0.02, p=0.95), ruling out a trivial power confound. We report this contrast as a finding in its own right: whether a cluster-mean ranking looks "unstable" against a spatial-graph-explicit alternative can itself depend on the upstream cell-typing scheme — a variable CCC benchmarking studies do not typically vary or report, and should.

**Foundation-model negative control (scGPT zero-shot).** Zero-shot scGPT embeddings reach silhouette 0.136 against the existing cell-type labels, versus 0.148 for a 50-component PCA baseline on the same 274 genes and cells (ratio 0.92, narrowly clearing a pre-registered ≥0.90 quality gate; scGPT did not outperform PCA). The resulting embedding-similarity score, aggregated over the Null-model-2 edges, does not correlate with edge z-score (Spearman ρ≈−0.002, p≈0.99 across all 22 combinations), and CD274→PDCD1 pairs — zero physical contact — show *high* embedding similarity (0.89–0.93), indistinguishable from CXCL12→CXCR4 pairs: the spatial-graph-explicit score captures a directional, contact-dependent signal a general-purpose zero-shot embedding does not recover on its own.

## Related Work and Novelty

Compositional cluster-mean scoring underlies the most widely used CCC tools, including CellChat (Jin et al. 2021, *Nat. Commun.*, nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21246-9), NicheNet (Browaeys et al. 2020, *Nat. Methods*, pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31819264), and LIANA+ (Dimitrov et al. 2024, *Nat. Cell Biol.*, nature.com/articles/s41556-024-01469-w), none of which condition significance testing on the spatial adjacency graph by default. Spatially-explicit alternatives exist — COMMOT (Cang et al. 2023, *Nat. Methods*, optimal transport, nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01728-4), SpatialDM (Li et al. 2023, *Nat. Commun.*, bivariate Moran's I, nature.com/articles/s41467-023-39608-w), and SPIDER (Li et al. 2025, *Nat. Commun.*, functional-support-augmented, nature.com/articles/s41467-025-62988-0) — but to our knowledge none directly quantifies, via a matched-permutation ablation on the same interactions, how much a cluster-mean ranking misrepresents locally-supported communication relative to a spatially-explicit test. That controlled comparison, isolating only the spatial-adjacency term while holding the permutation engine, seed, and interaction list fixed, is the contribution here. *Novelty status: potential gap — the general critique that compositional scores ignore spatial structure is established in the field; the specific quantified rank-instability comparison requires a final check against recent CCC benchmarking literature (e.g., saezlab/liana benchmark suites) before camera-ready submission.*

## Evaluation Plan

Current results are from a single 2-FOV tissue section (structural limitation: no cross-patient replicate to test whether rank instability generalizes). Planned for the full paper: (1) replicate the compositional-vs-edge-null comparison across a multi-patient spatial cohort to test reproducibility of the rank shift; (2) add a detection-rate-gated significance filter as a direct ablation baseline; (3) benchmark directly against COMMOT and SpatialDM on the same dataset and interactions.
