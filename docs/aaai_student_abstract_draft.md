## Abstract

Cell–cell communication (CCC) inference in spatial transcriptomics is typically ranked using cluster-mean ligand–receptor (L–R) scores that never reference the spatial graph. On a 10x Xenium human breast dataset (2 testable L–R pairs of 20 curated: CXCL12–CXCR4, CD274–PDCD1), compositional scoring manufactures significance for interactions with **zero physical spatial support**: every CD274→PDCD1 combination flagged significant compositionally (4/4 per-cell typing, 6/6 Leiden-consensus typing) collapses to an edge score of exactly 0.000 under a spatial-edge null — not one of 42,978 directed edges connects a CD274-expressing cell to a PDCD1-expressing cell, in either typing scheme, surviving BH-FDR correction (q≤0.003 vs. q≥0.05). CXCL12→CXCR4 shows instability that is itself typing-dependent: rank 1→8 of 16 (89% z-drop) under per-cell typing, but only rank 2→3 of 9 under Leiden-consensus typing. A zero-shot scGPT baseline narrowly clears a PCA quality gate but does not recover the spatial-contact signal (ρ≈0); two further GNN attempts (self-supervised link prediction, confidence-filtered rescoring) also fail pre-registered thresholds. Across four independent representation-learning attempts, none recovers or improves on the direct spatial-edge statistic. CCC pipelines should report significance under spatially-explicit nulls, and rank-instability claims should be checked against more than one cell-typing scheme before being treated as general.

## Motivation

The dominant CCC scoring convention — CellPhoneDB-style cluster-mean expression products, as in `squidpy.gr.ligrec` (Palla et al. 2022) — computes a statistic mathematically identical to non-spatial scRNA-seq: it averages expression within cell-type clusters and never touches the spatial graph, creating a validity gap between what the score claims to measure (spatially-supported communication) and what it measures (cluster co-expression, independent of location). We quantify this gap with a controlled ablation — same interactions, same permutation engine, differing only in whether the statistic conditions on the spatial graph — and test whether *which* interactions look unstable is itself robust to cell-typing method, a variable rarely treated as experimental in CCC benchmarking.

## Data and Methods

**Panel coverage (scope statement).** Of 20 curated L–R pairs, only **2** — CXCL12–CXCR4 and CD274–PDCD1 — have both ligand and receptor on the 10x Xenium 280-gene targeted breast panel; the other 18 were untestable a priori (a hard panel-design constraint, not a post hoc filter). All results below concern these 2 pairs (22 combinations under per-cell typing, 18 under Leiden-consensus).

**Dataset.** Public 10x Genomics Xenium Human Breast panel, 2-FOV bundle (CC BY 4.0). *Scope caveat:* 10x documents this file as intended only for format-validation, explicitly "not meant to be used to draw biological conclusions" (10x Genomics, ref. 8) — a trimmed subsample of 10x's full Human Breast preview dataset (167,780 cells); effect sizes below are a single-section proof of concept pending replication (Evaluation Plan). After QC (min_counts=10, min_cells=3): 7,163 cells × 280 genes. Spatial graph: squidpy k-NN, k=6, no self-loops (42,978 directed edges).

**Two independent cell-typing schemes.** (1) *Per-cell*: marker-score argmax, 4 types (epithelial 5,355; fibroblast 1,398; immune 361; endothelial 49). (2) *Leiden-consensus*: cluster-level consensus at resolution 0.5, yielding 3 types after endothelial cells (n=49) are absorbed into larger clusters (epithelial 5,756; immune 773; fibroblast 634); resolution was not tuned to force endothelial separation.

**Baseline ranking.** The 20 curated L–R pairs, scored with `squidpy.gr.ligrec`, yield 22 nonzero sender–receiver combinations under per-cell typing (16 CXCL12→CXCR4, 6 CD274→PDCD1) and 18 under Leiden-consensus typing (9 each).

**Null model 1 (compositional) vs. Null model 2 (spatial-edge).** Both use the same 500 label permutations (seed=42), preserving cluster sizes. Null 1 is the cluster-mean L–R product (never queries the spatial graph). Null 2 computes the same statistic only over spatial-graph edges (i→j) whose permuted labels match sender/receiver type, averaging L(i)×R(j) over those edges — the only difference between the nulls, isolating the effect of conditioning on adjacency; both re-run under per-cell and Leiden-consensus typing.

**Multiple-testing correction.** BH-FDR correction was computed from empirical p-values across the 22 per-cell-typing tests, separately per null.

**Negative controls (embedding + graph-learning).** Pretrained scGPT "whole-human" (Cui et al. 2024), run zero-shot on the 274/280 panel genes in vocabulary, embeds all cells to derive a per-pair similarity score (mean cosine over Null-2 edges). A 2-layer GraphSAGE (Hamilton et al. 2017; hidden 128, output 64) was trained as a self-supervised link predictor (85/15 split, seed=42) on scGPT or PCA-50 node features, gated at AUC≥0.75; the passing decoder gave a learned score per pair, with a confidence-filtered variant restricting the true L–R product to each pair's top-50%-confidence edges before recomputing the null.

**Reproducibility.** Reruns produce byte-identical hashes; sub-floor p-values are reported as p<0.002, not p=0.000.

## Results

**Primary finding: CD274→PDCD1 zero-spatial-contact is typing-invariant.** Under compositional scoring, CD274→PDCD1 is flagged significant (p<0.05) in 4/6 per-cell and 6/6 Leiden-consensus combinations. Under the spatial-edge null, every one collapses to an edge score of exactly 0.000 (p=1.000) in **both** typing schemes: not one of 42,978 directed edges connects a CD274-expressing cell to a PDCD1-expressing cell. This traces to PDCD1's near-absent detection (5/7,163 cells, 0.07%). BH-FDR correction confirms all 9 verdict flips survive at q≤0.003 compositionally, while edge-null q≥0.05 throughout (median q=1.00) — not a multiple-testing artifact.

**Secondary finding: CXCL12→CXCR4 rank instability is itself sensitive to cell-typing choice.** Under per-cell typing, the top compositional interaction, fibroblast→epithelial (z=42.9), falls to rank 8/16 under the spatial-edge null (z=4.6, 89% reduction), and 5/16 lose significance entirely (ρ=0.79 compositional-vs-edge correlation, p=0.0002). Under Leiden-consensus typing, the same interaction shows only a mild shift (rank 2→3 of 9), 0/9 lose significance, and the correlation is tighter (ρ=0.97). Edge count does not predict edge z-score (ρ=0.02, p=0.95), ruling out a power confound. Rank instability against a spatial-graph-explicit alternative can itself depend on the upstream cell-typing scheme — a variable CCC benchmarking rarely varies or reports.

**Foundation-model negative control (scGPT zero-shot).** Zero-shot scGPT embeddings reach silhouette 0.136 vs. 0.148 for a 50-component PCA baseline (ratio 0.92, narrowly clearing a pre-registered ≥0.90 gate; scGPT did not outperform PCA). The embedding-similarity score does not correlate with edge z-score (ρ≈−0.002, p≈0.99), and CD274→PDCD1 pairs — zero physical contact — show *high* similarity (0.89–0.93), indistinguishable from CXCL12→CXCR4: the spatial-edge score captures a contact-dependent signal zero-shot embeddings do not recover.

**Graph-learning negative controls (two variants).** The scGPT-features link predictor failed the pre-registered gate (AUC=0.70 vs. 0.75); the identical pipeline with PCA-50 features passed cleanly (AUC=0.98), isolating the failure to scGPT's representation. The passing model's learned score was, on inspection, ligand/receptor-blind by construction — numerically identical across L–R pairs whenever sender/receiver types matched (e.g., fibroblast→fibroblast: 0.9362 for both pairs), satisfying only 1 of 3 pre-registered criteria. A gene-aware fix (filtering to top-50%-confidence edges before recomputing the real L–R product) passed a correctness gate (max diff 1.8×10⁻¹⁵) and preserved ranking (ρ=0.95) but did not sharpen top hits (median |z| 7.5→6.0); CD274→PDCD1 stayed at 0 (sanity check only). Together with scGPT, three independent representation-learning attempts converge: none outperforms the direct spatial-edge test.

## Related Work and Novelty

Compositional cluster-mean scoring underlies CellChat (Jin et al. 2021, nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21246-9), NicheNet (Browaeys et al. 2020, pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31819264), and LIANA+ (Dimitrov et al. 2024, nature.com/articles/s41556-024-01469-w), none conditioning significance on the spatial graph by default. Spatially-explicit alternatives exist — COMMOT (Cang et al. 2023, nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01728-4), SpatialDM (Li et al. 2023, nature.com/articles/s41467-023-39608-w), SPIDER (Li et al. 2025, nature.com/articles/s41467-025-62988-0) — but none quantifies via matched-permutation ablation how much a cluster-mean ranking misrepresents locally-supported communication, nor tests whether learned/foundation-model representations can substitute for explicit graph conditioning. *Novelty status: potential gap — the spatial-structure critique is established; the quantification and negative controls require a final literature check before camera-ready.*

## Evaluation Plan

Current results are from a single, non-biological-conclusion-designated 2-FOV section (no cross-patient replicate). Planned, in priority order: (1) replicate the core null comparison on 10x's full Human Breast dataset (167,780 cells) or an independent Xenium cohort, to confirm findings are not an artifact of the trimmed subset; (2) add a detection-rate-gated significance filter as an ablation baseline; (3) benchmark against COMMOT and SpatialDM on the same data.
